Reduced a 2-day, 5-person manual data reconciliation job down to 1 person in 1–2 hours. Two programs handle the heavy lifting — detecting mismatches and syncing them — while one operator stays in control of the process.

How It Works

Program 1 — Comparison: Reads the spreadsheet and scrapes the office website, then outputs a report of every record that is missing or needs to be updated — one operator reviews and confirms before anything changes
Program 2 — Sync: Takes the confirmed comparison output and automatically inserts missing records and updates stale ones directly on the website — operator stays in the loop but does no manual data entry
Semi-automated by design — the programs do the repetitive work, the operator keeps oversight and control
